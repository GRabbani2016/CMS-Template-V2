ISLAMIC CENTER OF DELTONA

Your Contribution Creates Generations of Worship Tomorrow

The Islamic Center of Deltona is a welcoming masjid and community center serving Muslims in Deltona, DeBary, Orange City, Sanford and nearby Central Florida communities.

Located at 420 Summerhaven Dr. DeBary, FL 32713, our masjid offers five daily prayers, Friday Jumu'ah prayer, Islamic classes, community gatherings, Ramadan programs, Eid salah, Janaza services, and burial support through the graveyard next to the masjid.

Join us for prayer, learning, brotherhood, sisterhood and service to the community.
